# Post-change audit: intervention comparison and AI states

Scope: the requested scenario comparison, selected-location freshness label, AI
states, backend recovery, responsive action rows and README accuracy. This is a
bounded audit of these changes, not a certification of every existing screen.
The user selected audit-after mode. No additional redesign is proposed.

The existing direction is `frontend/DESIGN.md`: pale blue glass civic dashboard,
navy text and teal actions. Map geography and the concentration grid retain their
purpose. No visual assets or navigation destinations were created.

## Findings

No additional actionable findings in the reviewed changes after verification.
Population and zones remain synthetic; source hypotheses and intervention
sensitivity remain modeled assumptions. Their labels are part of the interface.
